#几何 

>[!def] 余切映射
>设$M,N$是[[微分流形|光滑流形]], $p\in M$, [[切映射]]$\d\phi|_p:T_pM\to T_{\phi(p)}N$所诱导[[余切空间]]之间的线性映射
>$$\d^*\phi|_p:T^*_{\phi(p)}N\to T^*_{p}M$$
>定义为
>$$\d^*\phi|_p(\alpha)(v)=\alpha(\d\phi|_p(v))$$
>即使下图表交换
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\large
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] {T_{\phi(p)}N} & {\mathbb R} & \alpha & {T^*_{\phi(p)}N} \\ {T_pM} && {\mathrm d^*\phi|_p(\alpha)} & {T^*_pM} \arrow["\alpha", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-3, to=1-4] \arrow[maps to, from=1-3, to=2-3] \arrow["{\mathrm d^*\phi|_p}", from=1-4, to=2-4] \arrow["{\mathrm d\phi|_p}", from=2-1, to=1-1] \arrow["{\mathrm d^*\phi|_p(\alpha)}"', from=2-1, to=1-2] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=2-3, to=2-4] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>称$\d^*\phi|_p$为**余切映射**(cotangent map). 注意它和切映射方向相反.
