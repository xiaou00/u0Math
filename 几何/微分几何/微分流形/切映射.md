#几何 

>[!def] 切映射
>设$M,N$是[[微分流形|光滑流形]], $\phi:M\to N$是[[光滑映射]], $p\in M$, 那么$\phi$可以诱导对应点[[切空间]]之间的线性映射. 首先显然有$\phi$诱导的$\csp^\infty_{\phi(p)}$到$\csp^\infty_p$的映射$\phi^*$定义为
>$$\phi^*(g)=g\circ\phi,\quad\forall g\in\csp^\infty_{\phi(p)}$$
>即下列图表交换
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] N & {\mathbb R} \\ M & {\mathbb R}
\arrow[""{name=0, anchor=center, inner sep=0}, "{g\in\mathbf{C}^\infty_{\phi(p)}}", from=1-1, to=1-2]
\arrow[equal, from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["\phi", from=2-1, to=1-1]
\arrow[""{name=1, anchor=center, inner sep=0}, "{g\circ\phi\in\mathbf{C}^\infty_p}"', dashed, from=2-1, to=2-2]
\arrow["{\phi^*}",Rightarrow,from=0, to=1] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>容易验证$\phi^*(g)\in\csp^\infty_p$. 这样对任意切向量$v\in T_pM$可以定义映射
>$$\d\phi|_p(v):\csp^\infty_{\phi(p)}\to\R$$
>使得对任意$g\in\csp^\infty_{\phi(p)}$有
>$$\d\phi|_p(v)(g)=v(\phi^*(g))=v(g\circ\phi)$$
>显然$\d\phi|_p(v):\csp^\infty_{\phi(x)}\to\R$是$N$在点$\phi(p)$处的一个[[切向量]], 于是映射
>$$\d\phi|_p:T_pM\to T_{\phi(p)}N,\quad v\mapsto\d\phi_p(v)$$
>是线性映射, 称为光滑映射$\phi$在点$p$诱导的**切映射**(tangent map).

关于$\d\phi|_p$是线性映射的证明是明显的, 设$u,v\in T_pM,g\in\csp^\infty_{\phi(p)}$, 则
$$
\begin{aligned}
\d\phi|_p(u+v)(g)=&(u+v)(g\circ\phi)\\
=&u(g\circ\phi)+v(g\circ \phi)\\
=&\d\phi|_p(u)(g)+\d\phi|_p(v)(g)\\
=&(\d\phi|_p(u)+\d\phi|_p(v))(g)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\d\phi|_p(\lambda u)(g)=&(\lambda u)(g\circ\phi)\\
=&\lambda\cdot u(g\circ\phi)\\
=&(\lambda\cdot\d\phi|_p(u))(g)
\end{aligned}
$$
其中$\lambda\in\R$. 因此$\d\phi|_p$是线性的. 由于线性映射由其在基向量上的取值完全决定, 设$\phi:M\to N$将$p$的局部坐标系$(U;x^i)$对应到$\phi(p)$的局部坐标系$(V;y^j)$. 那么光滑映射$\phi:M\to N$可以局部表为
$$y^j=y^j(x^1,\ldots,x^m)$$
其中每个$y^i(x^1,\ldots,x^m)$都是$U\cap\phi^{-1}(V)$上的光滑函数, 在切空间$T_pM$和$T_{\phi(p)}N$中分别有自然基底$\set{\dfrac{\p}{\p x^i}}$和$\set{\dfrac{\p}{\p y^j}}$. 设
$$\d\phi|_p\left(\frac{\p}{\p x^i}\right)=\sum_{n=1}^n{A^j_i\frac{\p}{\p y^j}}$$
上式两边都作用在坐标函数$y^k$上, 得
$$A^k_i=\left(\d\phi|_p\left(\frac{\p}{\p x^i}\right)\right)(y^k)=\frac{\p}{\p x^i}(y^k\circ\phi)=\frac{\p y^k}{\p x^i}$$
因此
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{\p y^1}{\p x^1}&\dfrac{\p y^2}{\p x^1}&\cdots&\dfrac{\p y^n}{\p x^1}\\
\dfrac{\p y^1}{\p x^2}&\dfrac{\p y^2}{\p x^2}&\cdots&\dfrac{\p y^n}{\p x^2}\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
\dfrac{\p y^1}{\p x^m}&\dfrac{\p y^2}{\p x^m}&\cdots&\dfrac{\p y^n}{\p x^m}\\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
恰好对应$\phi$局部坐标表达式的[[Jacobi矩阵]]. 

切映射还有另一者表示方式, 设$v\in T_pM$, 则在$M$中有一条光滑曲线$\gamma:(-\epsilon,\epsilon)\to M$使得$\gamma(0)=p$并且$\gamma'(0)=v$, 那么
$$\d\phi|_p(v)=\d\phi|_p(\gamma'(0))=\dd t{}\Big|_{t=0}\phi\circ\gamma(t)$$
即
$$\d\phi|_p\left(\dd t{}\Big|_{t=0}\gamma(t)\right)=\dd t{}\Big|_{t=0}\phi\circ\gamma(t)$$
此处$\phi\circ\gamma:(-\epsilon,\epsilon)\to N$是光滑流形$N$中过$\phi(p)$的一条光滑曲线.
