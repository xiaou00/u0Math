#范畴论

\renewcommand{\mor}[1]{\operatorname{Mor}{\mathfrak{#1}}}
\renewcommand{\dom}[1]{\operatorname{dom}{#1}}
\renewcommand{\cod}[1]{\operatorname{cod}{#1}}
\newcommand{\sub}[1]{\operatorname{Sub}{(#1)}}
\newcommand{\quot}[1]{\operatorname{Quot}{(#1)}}$$
>[!def] 伴随函子
>设$\frak A,B$是[[范畴]], $F:\frak A\to B$, $G:\frak B\to A$是一对[[函子]], 考虑下面两个[[双函子]]:
>$$\begin{aligned}\mathfrak{A}^{\rm op}\times\mathfrak{B}\to\mathsf{Set}:(A,B)\mapsto\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\\ \mathfrak{A}^{\rm op}\times\mathfrak{B}\to\mathsf{Set}:(A,B)\mapsto\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))\end{aligned}$$
>若这两个函子之间存在[[自然变换|自然同构]], 也就是说存在双函子同构
>$$\Hom_\mathfrak{A}(-,G(-))\simeq\Hom_\mathfrak{B}(F(-),-)$$
>则称$F$与$G$是一对**伴随函子**(adjoint functor pair), $F$是$G$的**左伴随**(left adjoint), $G$是$F$的**右伴随**(right adjoint). 记作$(F\dashv G):\frak A\to B$.

上述定义也可以等价地转述为: 对任意的$A\in\ob{\frak A}$, $B\in\ob{\frak B}$, 都存在一个双射
$$\varphi_{A,B}:\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\to\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))$$
满足对于:
- $\frak A$中的任意态射$k:A'\to A$,
- $\frak B$中的任意态射$h:B\to B'$,
下面两个图表交换:
```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\Large\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small] {g:F(A)\to B} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)} & {\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))} & {f:A\to G(B)} \\ {g\circ F(k):F(A')\to B} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A'),B)} & {\mathfrak{A}(A',G(B))} & {f\circ k:A'\to G(B)} \\ {g:F(A)\to B} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)} & {\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))} & {f:A\to G(B)} \\ {h\circ g:F(A)\to B'} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B')} & {\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B'))} & {G(h)\circ f:A\to G(B')} \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{-\circ F(k)}", maps to, from=1-1, to=2-1] \arrow["{{\varphi_{A,B}}}", from=1-2, to=1-3] \arrow[from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow[from=1-3, to=2-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-4, to=1-3] \arrow["{-\circ k}"', maps to, from=1-4, to=2-4] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=2-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{{\varphi_{A',B}}}"', from=2-2, to=2-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=2-4, to=2-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=3-1, to=3-2] \arrow["{h\circ-}", maps to, from=3-1, to=4-1] \arrow["{{\varphi_{A,B}}}", from=3-2, to=3-3] \arrow[from=3-2, to=4-2] \arrow[from=3-3, to=4-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=3-4, to=3-3] \arrow["{G(h)\circ-}"', maps to, from=3-4, to=4-4] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=4-1, to=4-2] \arrow["{{\varphi_{A,B'}}}"', from=4-2, to=4-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=4-4, to=4-3] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
```

也就是满足自然变换的条件 (同构由双射性确保).

>[!ex] 例1
>设$F:\sf Set\to Grp$是[[自由群]]函子, $G:\sf Grp\to Set$是[[遗忘函子]], 则$F\dashv G$.

>[!pf]
>我们尝试构造自然同构
>$$\varphi_{S,A}:\mathsf{Grp}(F(S),A)\cong\mathsf{Set}(S,G(A))$$
>其中$S\in\ob{\sf Set}, A\in\ob{\sf Grp}$. 一个显然的想法是利用自由群的泛性质 ([[自由群|此页面定理1]]).
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] S & {F(S)} \\ {G(A)} & A \arrow["F", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["f"', from=1-1, to=2-1] \arrow["\alpha", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["G", from=2-2, to=2-1] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>考虑给定$f\in\mathsf{Set}(S,G(A))$, 由自由群的泛性质(上图交换)立即知存在唯一的群同态$\alpha:F(S)\to A$. 
>反之考虑给定群同态$\alpha:F(S)\to A$ ($\alpha\in\mathsf{Grp}(F(S),A)$), 考虑$S$上的典范嵌入$\iota_S:S\to F(S)$, 将$S$中元素视作$F(S)$的生成元. 将$\alpha$限制到$S$上, 由于$S\subset F(S)$, 且$G(A)$是$A$的底层集合, 因此$G(\alpha)\circ\iota_S$ (还是上图交换)就确定了集合映射$f$. 这就证明了
>$$f=\varphi_{S,A}(\alpha)=G(\alpha)\circ\iota_S:S\xrightarrow{\iota_S}{F(S)}\xrightarrow{\alpha}{A}\xrightarrow{G}{G(A)},\quad\varphi^{-1}_{S,A}(f)=\alpha$$
>显然$\varphi_{S,A}$是双射. 下面证明对于$k:S'\to S\in\mathsf{Set}(S',S)$和$h:A\to A'\in\mathsf{Grp}(A,A')$, 下图交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] {\mathsf{Grp}(F(S),A)} & {\mathsf{Set}(S,G(A))} \\ {\mathsf{Grp}(F(S'),A)} & {\mathsf{Set}(S',G(A))} \\ {\mathsf{Grp}(F(S),A)} & {\mathsf{Set}(S,G(A))} \\ {\mathsf{Grp}(F(S),A')} & {\mathsf{Set}(S,G(A'))} \arrow["{\varphi_{S,A}}", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{-\circ F(k)}"{description}, from=2-1, to=1-1] \arrow["{\varphi_{S',A}}", from=2-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{-\circ k}"{description}, from=2-2, to=1-2] \arrow["{\varphi_{S,A}}", from=3-1, to=3-2] \arrow["{h\circ -}"{description}, from=3-1, to=4-1] \arrow["{G(h)\circ -}"{description}, from=3-2, to=4-2] \arrow["{\varphi_{S',A}}", from=4-1, to=4-2] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>对于前者, 由于自由函子$F$保持典范映射$\iota$, 即典范映射$\iota$是一个自然变换, 因此
>$$(\alpha\circ F(k))\circ \iota_S=\alpha'\circ(F(k)\circ\iota_S)=\alpha'\circ(\iota_{S'}\circ k)=(\alpha'\circ\iota_{S'})\circ k$$
>从而图表交换. 对于后者, 由于$h$是群同态, 其作用于$A$的元素, $G(h)$只是$h$作为集合映射的表示, 即
>$$(h\circ\alpha)\circ\iota_S=h\circ(\alpha\circ\iota_S)=G(h)\circ(\alpha\circ\iota_S)$$
>这就证明了$\varphi_{S,A}$是双射且满足两个自然条件. 证毕.

>[!ex] 例2
>设$D:\sf Set\to Top$是一个函子使得对每个集合$X$, $D(X)$为以$X$为底集赋予离散拓扑的空间. $U:\sf Top\to Set$是遗忘函子, 则$D\dashv U$.
>设$T:\sf Set\to Top$是赋予密着拓扑的拓扑空间, 则$T$是$U$的右伴随$T\vdash U$.

下面是伴随性的一个等价定义:

>[!thm] 定理1
>设$F:\frak A\to B$, $G:\frak B\to A$是一对函子, $F\dashv G$当且仅当存在两个自然变换
>$$\eta:1_{\frak A}\Rightarrow GF,\quad \epsilon:FG\Rightarrow 1_{\frak B}$$
>满足$(G\epsilon)(\eta G)=1_G$以及$(\epsilon F)(F\eta)=1_F$, 即下图表交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] G & GFG & F & FGF \\ & G && F \arrow["{\eta G}", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{1_G}"', from=1-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{G\epsilon}", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["{F\eta}", from=1-3, to=1-4] \arrow["{1_F}"', from=1-3, to=2-4] \arrow["{\epsilon F}", from=1-4, to=2-4] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```

>[!pf]
>若$F\dashv G$, 则对任意$A\in\ob{\frak A}$, $B\in\ob{\frak B}$, 那么存在一个双射
>$$\varphi_{A,B}:\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\to\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))$$
>令$\eta_A:A\to GF(A)$为
>$$\eta_A=\varphi_{A,F(A)}(1_{F(A)})$$
>$\epsilon_B:FG(B)\to B$为
>$$\epsilon_B=\varphi^{-1}_{G(B),B}(1_{G(B)})$$
>首先我们证明$\eta:A\mapsto(\eta_A:A\to GF(A))$和$\epsilon:B\mapsto(\epsilon_B:FG(B)\to B)$都是自然变换.
>设$h:A\to A'$, 欲证下方形交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] A & {GF(A)} \\ {A'} & {GF(A')} \arrow["{\eta_A}", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["h"', from=1-1, to=2-1] \arrow["{GF(h)}", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["{\eta_{A'}}"', from=2-1, to=2-2] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>只需考虑下交换图表:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small] & {1_{F(A)}} & {\eta_A} \\ {\sigma:F(A)\to F(A)} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),F(A))} & {\mathfrak{A}(A,GF(A))} & {\sigma':A\to GF(A)} \\ {F(h)\circ\sigma:F(A)\to F(A')} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),F(A'))} & {\mathfrak{A}(A,GF(A'))} & {GF(h)\circ \sigma':A\to GF(A')} \\ {\tau\circ F(h):F(A)\to F(A')} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),F(A'))} & {\mathfrak{A}(A,GF(A'))} & {\tau'\circ h:A\to GF(A')} \\ {\tau:F(A')\to F(A')} & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A'),F(A'))} & {\mathfrak{A}(A',GF(A'))} & {\tau':A'\to GF(A')} \\ & {1_{F(A')}} & {\eta_{A'}} \arrow[maps to, from=1-2, to=1-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-3, to=2-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=2-1, to=2-2] draw=none, from=2-4, to=2-3] \arrow["{GF(h)\circ -}", maps to, from=2-4, to=3-4] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=3-1, to=3-2] \arrow["{{\varphi_{A,F(A')}}}"', from=3-2, to=3-3] \arrow[equal, from=3-2, to=4-2] \arrow[equal, from=3-3, to=4-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=3-4, to=3-3] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, >
>由第一个交换方形(上半部分)有
>$$GF(h)\circ \eta_A=GF(h)\circ \varphi_{A,F(A)}( 1_{F(A)})=\varphi_{A,F(A')}\circ F(h)$$
>由第二个交换方形(下半部分)有
>$$\varphi_{A,F(A')}\circ F(h)=\varphi_{A',F(A')}(1_{F(A'})\circ h=\eta_{A'}\circ h$$
>这就证明了$\eta:1_{\mathfrak A}\Rightarrow GF$是自然变换, 类似可证$\epsilon:FG\Rightarrow 1_{\frak B}$是自然变换.
>
>欲证$(G\epsilon)(\eta G)=1_G$, 对每个$B\in\ob{\frak B}$, 由$\epsilon_B:FG(B)\to B$出发, 考虑下交换方形即可
>```tikz

\end{document}
>```
> 类似地可以证明$(F\eta)(\epsilon F)=1_F$.
> 
>---
>反之, 若存在自然变换$\eta:1_{\frak A}\Rightarrow GF$与$\epsilon:FG\Rightarrow 1_{\frak B}$满足$(G\epsilon)(\eta G)=1_G$, $(\epsilon F)(F\eta)=1_F$, 设$A\in\ob{\frak A}, B\in\ob{\frak{B}}$. 令$\varphi_{A,B}:\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\to\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))$使得对任意的$g:F(A)\to B$, $\varphi_{A,B}(g)$为复合
>$$A\xrightarrow{\eta_A}{GF(A)}\xrightarrow{G(g)}{G(B)}$$
>同时令$\theta_{A,B}:\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))\to\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)$使得对任意$h:A\to G(B)$, $\theta_{A,B}(h)$为复合
>$$F(A)\xrightarrow{F(h)}{FG(B)}\xrightarrow{\epsilon_B}B$$
>从而有$\epsilon$的自然变换性质, 以及$(\epsilon F)(F\eta)=1_F$有
>$$\begin{aligned}
\theta_{A,B}\circ\varphi_{A,B}(g)&=\epsilon_BFG(g)F(\eta_A)\\
&=g\epsilon_{F(A)}F(\eta_A)\\
&=g1_{F(A)}=g
\end{aligned}$$
>因此$\theta_{A,B}\circ\varphi_{A,B}$是恒同的, 同理$\varphi_{A,B}\circ\theta_{A,B}$是恒同的, 从而$\varphi_{A,B}:\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\to\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))$为双射, 显然由 $\eta$ 和 $\epsilon$ 的自然性可以验证$\varphi$的自然性.

由上述定理, 我们可以定义下面的概念:

>[!def] 单位&余单位
>设$F:\frak A\to B$, $G:\frak B\to A$是一对伴随函子$F\dashv G$, 那么存在两个自然变换
>$$\eta:1_{\frak A}\Rightarrow GF,\quad \epsilon:FG\Rightarrow 1_{\frak B}$$
>满足$(G\epsilon)(\eta G)=1_G$以及$(\epsilon F)(F\eta)=1_F$, 即下图表交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] G & GFG & F & FGF \\ & G && F \arrow["{\eta G}", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{1_G}"', from=1-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{G\epsilon}", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["{F\eta}", from=1-3, to=1-4] \arrow["{1_F}"', from=1-3, to=2-4] \arrow["{\epsilon F}", from=1-4, to=2-4] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>其中$\eta$称为该伴随的**单位**(unit)$\epsilon$称为该伴随的**余单位**(counit). 显然函子的伴随性由单位和余单位完全确定.

>[!thm] 定理2 (伴随的等价定义)
>设$F:\frak A\to B$, $G:\frak B\to A$是一对函子, 则下列命题等价:
>1. $F\dashv G$.
>2. 存在一个自然变换$\eta:1_{\frak A}\Rightarrow GF$使得对任意$A\in\ob{\frak A}$, $\eta_A:A\to GF(A)$满足$G$对$A$的万有性质:
>	对任意的$f:A\to G(B)$存在唯一的$g:F(A)\to B$使得$f=G(g)\eta_A$. 即下图表交换:
>	```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] A & {GF(A)} \\ {G(B)} \arrow["{\eta_A}", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["f"', from=1-1, to=2-1] \arrow["{G(g)}", dashed, from=1-2, to=2-1] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>	```
>3. 存在一个自然变换$\epsilon:FG\Rightarrow 1_{\frak B}$使得对任意$B\in\ob{\frak B}$, $\epsilon_B:FG(B)\to B$满足$F$对$B$的万有性质:
>	对任意的$h:F(A)\to B$, 存在唯一的$t:A\to G(B)$使得$h=\epsilon_B F(t)$, 即下图表交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[cramped,row sep=large] {FG(B)} & B \\ & {F(A)} \arrow["{\epsilon_B}", from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{F(t)}", dashed, from=2-2, to=1-1] \arrow["h"', from=2-2, to=1-2] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```

>[!pf]
>(1)⇒(2). 由定理1, 伴随对$F\dashv G$确定了一个单位$\eta:1_{\frak A}\to GF$, 对任意$A\in\ob{\frak A}$, 我们来验证$\eta_A:A\to GF(A)$满足$G$对$A$的万有性质.
>设$f:A\to F(B)$, 由伴随$F\dashv G$存在双射$\varphi_{A,B}:\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\to\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))$. 令$g:F(A)\to B$为$g=\varphi^{-1}_{A,B}(f)$, 考虑下交换方形:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd} {1_{F(A)}} &&&& {\eta_A} \\ & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),F(A))} && {\mathfrak{A}(A,GF(A))} \\ & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)} && {\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))} \\ g &&&& f \arrow[maps to, from=1-1, to=1-5] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{g\circ-}"', maps to, from=1-1, to=4-1] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-5, to=2-4] \arrow["{G(g)\circ-}", maps to, from=1-5, to=4-5] \arrow["{\varphi_{A,F(A)}}", from=2-2, to=2-4] \arrow[from=2-2, to=3-2] \arrow[from=2-4, to=3-4] \arrow["{\varphi_{A,B}}", from=3-2, to=3-4] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=4-1, to=3-2] \arrow[maps to, from=4-1, to=4-5] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=4-5, to=3-4] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>显然有$f=G(g)\eta_A$, 并且这样的$g$是唯一的.
>
>(2)⇒(1). 对任意的$A\in\ob{\frak A}$, $B\in\ob{\frak B}$, 令
>$$\varphi_{A,B}:\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)\to\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B)),\quad g\mapsto G(g)\eta_A$$
>显然由题设$\varphi_{A,B}$是双射. 
>另外对任意的$f:A'\to A$, 考虑$\eta$是自然变换, 容易验证下方形交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd} g &&&& {G(g)\eta_A} \\ & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A),B)} && {\mathfrak{A}(A,G(B))} \\ & {\mathfrak{B}(F(A'),B)} && {\mathfrak{A}(A',G(B))} \\ {g\circ F(f)} &&&& {G(g)\eta_A\circ f} \arrow[maps to, from=1-1, to=1-5] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{-\circ F(f)}"', maps to, from=1-1, to=4-1] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=1-5, to=2-4] \arrow["{-\circ f}", maps to, from=1-5, to=4-5] \arrow["{\varphi_{A,B}}", from=2-2, to=2-4] \arrow[from=2-2, to=3-2] \arrow[from=2-4, to=3-4] \arrow["{\varphi_{A',B}}", from=3-2, to=3-4] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=4-1, to=3-2] \arrow[maps to, from=4-1, to=4-5] \arrow["\in"{marking, allow upside down}, draw=none, from=4-5, to=3-4] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>对任意$h:B\to B'$, 由于$G$是函子, 下面方形交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{amsfonts}
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
