#范畴论 
$$\renewcommand{\ob}[1]{\operatorname{ob}{\mathfrak{#1}}}
\renewcommand{\mor}[1]{\operatorname{Mor}{\mathfrak{#1}}}
\renewcommand{\dom}[1]{\operatorname{dom}{#1}}
\renewcommand{\cod}[1]{\operatorname{cod}{#1}}
\newcommand{\sub}[1]{\operatorname{Sub}{(#1)}}
\newcommand{\quot}[1]{\operatorname{Quot}{(#1)}}$$
在一般的[[范畴]]中, 我们将两个对象之间全体态射视作[[集合]]$\Hom(A,B)$. 

>[!def] 充实范畴
>设$\mc V$是[[幺半范畴]], $\frak C$若满足:
>1. 对任意$x,y\in\ob{C}$, $\mathrm{Hom}_{\frak C}(x,y)\in\ob{\mc V}$.
>2. 对任意$x,y,z\in\ob{C}$, 存在$\mc V$中的复合态射
>$$\circ:\mathrm{Hom}_{\frak C}(y,z)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}_{\frak C}(x,y)\to \mathrm{Hom}_{\frak C}(x,z)$$
>3. 对每个$x\in\ob{C}$, 存在$\mc V$中的恒同态射
>$$1_x:\mb 1_\mc{V}\to\mathrm{Hom}_{\frak C}(x,x)$$
>
>并且满足下述条件 (图表交换):
>1. 单位律
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] & {\mathrm{Hom}(y,y)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)} \\ {\mathbf 1_{\mathcal V}\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)} & {\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)} & {\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)\otimes\mathbf 1_{\mathcal V}} \\ & {\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(x,x)} \arrow["\circ", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["{1_y\otimes1_{\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)}}", from=2-1, to=1-2] \arrow[equals, from=2-2, to=2-1] \arrow[equals, from=2-3, to=2-2] \arrow["{1_{\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)}\otimes 1_x}", from=2-3, to=3-2] \arrow["\circ", from=3-2, to=2-2] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>2. 结合律
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\Large
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] {\mathrm{Hom}(z,y)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(y,z)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)} & {\mathrm{Hom}(y,w)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)} \\ {\mathrm{Hom}(z,w)\otimes\mathrm{Hom}(x,z)} & {\mathrm{Hom}(x,w)} \arrow["{\circ\otimes1_{\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)}}"', from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{1_{\mathrm{Hom}(z,w)}\otimes\circ}"', from=1-1, to=2-1] \arrow["\circ", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["\circ"', from=2-1, to=2-2] \end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>则称范畴$\frak C$是**充实于$\mc V$的范畴**(enriched in $\mc V$). 或**$\mc V$-充实范畴**($\mc V$-enriched).
