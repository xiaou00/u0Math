#代数拓扑 

>[!def] 简约同调
>对于[[奇异同调|奇异链复形]]
>$$\cdots\to C_2(X)\xto{\p_2}C_1(X)\xto{\p_1}C_0(X)\to0$$
>我们定义
>$$\cdots\to C_2(X)\xto{\p_2}C_1(X)\xto{\p_1}C_0(X)\xto{\epsilon}\Z\to0$$
>其中
>$$\epsilon\left(\sum_i{n_i\sigma_i}\right)=\sum_in_i$$
>显然 $\epsilon\p_1=0$, 该复形的同调称为**简约同调**(reduced homology), 记作 $\widetilde H_n(X)$.

显然对于 $n>0$, $\widetilde H_n(X)\xto\sim H_n(X)$, $\widetilde H_0(X)\xto\sim\Z\oplus\widetilde H_0(X)$.

简约同调旨在简化 $H_0$ 的形式.
