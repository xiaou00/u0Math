#范畴论 

>[!def] 幺半范畴
>对于五元组$(\mc V,\otimes,a,\mathbb 1,\iota)$, 其中:
>1. $\mc V$是一个[[范畴]].
>2. $\otimes:\mc V\times\mc V\to\mc V$是[[双函子]], 其在对象和态射集上定义的映射记为$(X,Y)\mapsto X\otimes Y$和$(f,g)\mapsto f\otimes g$.
>3. $a$是[[函子范畴]]$\mathsf{Fct}(\mc V\times\mc V\times\mc V,\mc V)$中的[[同构]]:
>$$a:((\cdot\otimes\cdot)\otimes\cdot)\xrightarrow{\simeq}{(\cdot\otimes(\cdot\otimes\cdot))}$$
>使得对于所有对象$X,Y,Z,W$, 下图表交换:
>```tikz
>\usepackage{tikz-cd}
>\begin{document}
>\Large
>\begin{tikzcd} & {((X\otimes Y)\otimes Z)\otimes W} \\ \\ {(X\otimes(Y\otimes Z))\otimes W} && {(X\otimes Y)\otimes(Z\otimes W)} \\ \\ {X\otimes((Y\otimes Z)\otimes W)} && {X\otimes(Y\otimes(Z\otimes W))} \arrow["{a(X,Y,Z)\otimes 1_W}"', from=1-2, to=3-1] \arrow["{a(X\otimes Y,Z,W)}", from=1-2, to=3-3] \arrow["{a(X,Y\otimes Z,W)}"', from=3-1, to=5-1] \arrow["{a(X,Y,Z\otimes W)}", from=3-3, to=5-3] \arrow["{1_X\otimes a(Y,Z,W)}"', from=5-1, to=5-3] \end{tikzcd}
>\end{document}
>```
>也就是说满足结合律 (同构意义下).
>
>4. 对象$\mathbb 1\in\mathrm{ob}(\mc V)$称为幺元. 并且相应的函子$\mathbb 1\otimes -$和$-\otimes\mathbb 1$必然是$\mc V$到自身的[[范畴等价]].
>5. $\iota:\mathbb 1\otimes\mathbb 1\xrightarrow{\simeq}\mathbb 1$, 此为严格同构.
>
>则称$(\mc V,\otimes,a,\mathbb 1,\iota)$是一个**幺半范畴**(monodial category)或**张量范畴**(tensor category). $a$称之为**结合约束**. 运算$\otimes$也习惯上称之为**张量积**(tensor product).
