#代数 

>[!def] 双复形
>[[加性范畴]] $\mathfrak A$ 中的一族对象 $(X^{p,q})_{(p,q)\in\Z^2}$ 连同态射 $^{\rightarrow}d^{p,q}:X^{p,q}\to X^{p+1,q}$ 和 $^\uparrow d^{p,q}:X^{p,q}\to X^{p,q+1}$. 满足
>$$^\rightarrow d^{p+1,q} {^\rightarrow d}^{p,q}=0,\quad {^\uparrow}d^{p,q+1}{^\uparrow d}^{p,q}=0,\quad {^\rightarrow}d^{p,q+1}{^\uparrow d}^{p,q}={^\uparrow d}^{p+1,q}{^\rightarrow d}^{p,q}$$
>即下图表交换且每行每列都是复形:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \Large \begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] && \vdots && \vdots \\ \\ \cdots && {X^{p,q+1}} && {X^{p+1,q+1}} && \cdots \\ \\ \cdots && {X^{p,q}} && {X^{p+1,q}} && \cdots \\ \\ && \vdots && \vdots \arrow["{^\rightarrow d^{p-1,q+1}}"', from=3-1, to=3-3] \arrow["{^\uparrow d^{p,q+1}}", from=3-3, to=1-3] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d^{p,q+1}}"', from=3-3, to=3-5] \arrow["{^\uparrow d^{p+1,q+1}}", from=3-5, to=1-5] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d^{p+1,q+1}}"', from=3-5, to=3-7] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d^{p-1,q}}"', from=5-1, to=5-3] \arrow["{^\uparrow d^{p,q}}", from=5-3, to=3-3] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d^{p,q}}"', from=5-3, to=5-5] \arrow["{^\uparrow d^{p+1,q}}", from=5-5, to=3-5] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d^{p+1,q}}"', from=5-5, to=5-7] \arrow["{^\uparrow d^{p,q-1}}", from=7-3, to=5-3] \arrow["{^\uparrow d^{p+1,q-1}}", from=7-5, to=5-5] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
>```
>则称 $(X^{\bullet,\bullet},{^\rightarrow d}^{\bullet,\bullet},{^\uparrow d}^{\bullet,\bullet})$ 为一个**双复形**(double complex), 可简记作 $X^{\bullet,\bullet}$.

类似地可以定义态射和范畴

>[!def] 双复形范畴
>双复形之间的态射 $\Hom(X^{\bullet,\bullet},Y^{\bullet,\bullet})\ni f^{\bullet,\bullet}:X^{\bullet,\bullet}\to Y^{\bullet,\bullet}$ 意味着一组资料
>$$f^{\bullet,\bullet}=(f^{p,q}:X^{p,q}\to Y^{p,q})_{(p,q)\in\Z^2}$$
>使得对所有 $p,q$ 都有
>$${^\rightarrow d}^{p,q}_Y f^{p,q}=f^{p+1,q}{^\rightarrow}d^{p,q}_X,\quad{^\uparrow d}^{p,q}_Y f^{p,q}=f^{p,q+1}{^\uparrow}d^{p,q}_X$$
>即下图表交换:
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \Large \begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] & {Y^{p,q+1}} && {Y^{p+1,q+1}} \\ {X^{p,q+1}} && {X^{p+1,q+1}} \\ & {Y^{p,q}} && {Y^{p+1,q}} \\ {X^{p,q}} && {X^{p+1,q}} \arrow["{^\rightarrow d_Y^{p,q+1}}", from=1-2, to=1-4] \arrow["{f^{p,q+1}}"{pos=0.4}, from=2-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d_X^{p,q+1}}"'{pos=0.2}, from=2-1, to=2-3] \arrow["{f^{p+1,q+1}}"{pos=0.2}, from=2-3, to=1-4] \arrow["{^\uparrow d_Y^{p,q}}"'{pos=0.8}, from=3-2, to=1-2] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d_Y^{p,q}}"{pos=0.8}, from=3-2, to=3-4] \arrow["{^\uparrow d_Y^{p+1,q}}"', from=3-4, to=1-4] \arrow["{^\uparrow d_X^{p,q}}", from=4-1, to=2-1] \arrow["{f^{p,q}}", from=4-1, to=3-2] \arrow["{^\rightarrow d_X^{p,q}}"', from=4-1, to=4-3] \arrow["{^\uparrow d_X^{p+1,q}}"{pos=0.1}, from=4-3, to=2-3] \arrow["{f^{p+1,q}}"{pos=0.3}, from=4-3, to=3-4] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
>```
>这样构成的范畴记为 $\mathsf{Ch}^2(\mathfrak A)$.

对于一个双复形, 我们可以将其看作复形的复形, 这就导致了下面的定义

>[!def] 函子 $F_\mathrm{I},F_\mathrm{II}$
>设加性函子 $F_\mathrm{I}:\mathsf{Ch}^2(\mathfrak A)\to\mathsf{Ch}(\mathsf{Ch}(\mathfrak A))$ 定义如下: 对于双复形 $X^{\bullet,\bullet}$, 取 $$(F_\mathrm{I}X)^p=X^{p,\bullet}$$
>且
>$$d^p_{F_\mathrm{I}X}={^\rightarrow d}^{p,\bullet}:(F_\mathrm{I}X)^p\to(F_\mathrm{I}X)^{p+1}$$
>类似地,
>$$(F_\mathrm{II}X)^q=X^{\bullet,q}$$
>且 
>$$d^q_{F_\mathrm{II}X}={^\uparrow d}^{\bullet,q}:(F_\mathrm{II}X)^q\to(F_\mathrm{II}X)^{q+1}$$

可以比作双复形在两个方向上的投影:

```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \Large \begin{tikzcd} \vdots &&& \vdots \\ \\ {(F_\mathrm{II}X)^q} & \cdots && {X^{p,q}} && \cdots \\ \\ \vdots &&& \vdots \\ & \cdots && {(F_\mathrm{I}X)^p} && \cdots \arrow["{d^q_{F_\mathrm{II}X}}"', from=3-1, to=1-1] \arrow[from=3-2, to=3-4] \arrow["{{^\uparrow d}^{p,q}}", from=3-4, to=1-4] \arrow["{{^\rightarrow d}^{p,q}}"', from=3-4, to=3-6] \arrow[from=5-1, to=3-1] \arrow[from=5-4, to=3-4] \arrow[from=6-2, to=6-4] \arrow["{d^p_{F_\mathrm{I}X}}"', from=6-4, to=6-6] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
```
