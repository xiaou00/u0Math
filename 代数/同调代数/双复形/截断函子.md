#代数 

在同调代数中, 我们经常会遇到想要截断一个[[链复形]]的情形. 这类构造的核心直觉就是将链复形在某个特定的维度切断, 丢弃高维信息. 

对于一个复形 $X^\bullet$, 若我们直接定义截断
$$\sigma^{\le n}X^\bullet=\begin{cases}
X^i&i\le n\\
0&i>n
\end{cases}$$
然而, 这么做会破坏第 $n$ 阶的同调 $H^n(X)$. 所以我们定义以下四种截断:

>[!def] 截断函子
>设 $\mathfrak A$ 是[[Abel范畴]], $n\in\Z$, $X^\bullet$ 是给定复形, 令
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \large \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small] & {} \\ {\tau^{\le n}X} && \cdots & {X^{n-2}} & {X^{n-1}} & {\ker d^n} & 0 & 0 & \cdots \\ {\tilde\tau^{\le n}X} && \cdots & {X^{n-2}} & {X^{n-1}} & {X^n} & {\mathrm{coim}\,d^{n}} & 0 & \cdots \\ X && \cdots & {X^{n-2}} & {X^{n-1}} & {X^n} & {X^{n+1}} & {X^{n+2}} & \cdots \\ {\tilde\tau^{\ge n}X} && \cdots & 0 & {\mathrm{im}\, d^{n-1}} & {X^n} & {X^{n+1}} & {X^{n+2}} & \cdots \\ {\tau^{\ge n}X} && \cdots & 0 & 0 & {\mathrm{coker}\, d^{n-1}} & {X^{n+1}} & {X^{n+2}} & \cdots \\ & {} \arrow[hook, from=2-1, to=3-1] \arrow[from=2-3, to=2-4] \arrow[from=2-4, to=2-5] \arrow[from=2-5, to=2-6] \arrow[from=2-6, to=2-7] \arrow[hook, from=2-6, to=3-6] \arrow[from=2-7, to=2-8] \arrow[from=2-8, to=2-9] \arrow[hook, from=3-1, to=4-1] \arrow[from=3-3, to=3-4] \arrow[from=3-4, to=3-5] \arrow["{d^{n-1}}", from=3-5, to=3-6] \arrow[from=3-6, to=3-7] \arrow[from=3-7, to=3-8] \arrow[hook, from=3-7, to=4-7] \arrow[from=3-8, to=3-9] \arrow[two heads, from=4-1, to=5-1] \arrow[from=4-3, to=4-4] \arrow[from=4-4, to=4-5] \arrow["{d^{n-1}}", from=4-5, to=4-6] \arrow[two heads, from=4-5, to=5-5] \arrow["{d^n}", from=4-6, to=4-7] \arrow[from=4-7, to=4-8] \arrow[from=4-8, to=4-9] \arrow[two heads, from=5-1, to=6-1] \arrow[from=5-3, to=5-4] \arrow[from=5-4, to=5-5] \arrow[from=5-5, to=5-6] \arrow["{d^n}", from=5-6, to=5-7] \arrow[two heads, from=5-6, to=6-6] \arrow[from=5-7, to=5-8] \arrow[from=5-8, to=5-9] \arrow[from=6-3, to=6-4] \arrow[from=6-4, to=6-5] \arrow[from=6-5, to=6-6] \arrow[from=6-6, to=6-7] \arrow[from=6-7, to=6-8] \arrow[from=6-8, to=6-9] \arrow[no head, from=7-2, to=1-2] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
>```
>显然 $\tau^{\le n},\tilde\tau^{\le n},\tilde\tau^{\ge n},\tau^{\ge n}$ 都是[[加性函子]]. 并且 $\tau^{\le n}=[-n]\circ\tau^{\le 0}\circ[n]$, 其余亦然.

它是保持同调的, 即

>[!thm] 定理1
>有 $\mathfrak A$ 中的同构
>$$\begin{aligned}
&H^k(\tau^{\le n}X)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^k(\tilde\tau^{\le n}X)\simeq\begin{cases}
H^k(X)&k\le n\\
0&k>n
\end{cases}\\
&H^k(\tau^{\ge n}X)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^k(\tilde\tau^{\ge n}X)\simeq\begin{cases}
H^k(X)&k\ge n\\
0&k<n
\end{cases}\\
\end{aligned}$$
>并且有下列正合列
>$$\begin{aligned}
0\to\tilde\tau^{\le n-1}X\to\tau^{\le n}X\to H^n(X)[-n]\to 0\\
0\to H^n(X)[-n]\to\tau^{\ge n}X\to\tilde\tau^{\ge n+1}X\to 0\\
0\to\tau^{\le n}X\to X\to\tilde\tau^{\ge n+1}X\to0\\
0\to\tilde\tau^{\le n+1}X\to X\to\tau^{\ge n}X\to 0\\
0\to\tau^{\le n}X\to\tilde\tau^{\le n}X\to\mathrm{Cone}(\mathbb1_{\im d^n_X[-n-1]})\to0\end{aligned}$$
>此处的 $H^n(X)[-n]$ 视作复形.

>[!pf]
>参考图表, 态射 $\tilde\tau^{\le n-1}X\to\tau^{\le n}X$ 在 $n$ 次项是 $\im d^{n-1}\hookrightarrow\ker d^n$. 在其他项是 $\mathbb1$. 态射 $\tau^{\le n}X\to H^n(X)[-n]$ 在第 $n$ 项是
>$$\ker d^n\twoheadrightarrow\ker d^n/\im d^{n-1}=H^n(X)$$
>态射 $\tau^{\ge n}X\to\tilde\tau^{\ge n+1}X$ 在 $n$ 次项是 $\coker d^{n-1}\twoheadrightarrow\im d^n$, 在其他项为 $\mathbb 1$. 态射 $H^n(X)[-n]\to\tau^{\ge n}X$ 在第 $n$ 项是
>$$\ker d^n/\im d^{n-1}\hookrightarrow X^n/\im d^{n-1}=\coker d^{n-1}$$
>其余证明是平凡的.

>[!thm] 定理2
>对任何 $n\in\Z$, 有伴随
>$$\begin{aligned}
\Hom_{\mathsf{Ch}(\mathfrak A)}(X,Y)\simeq\Hom_{\mathsf{Ch}^{\le n}(\mathfrak A)}(X,\tau^{\le n}Y),\quad X\in\operatorname{ob}{\mathsf{Ch}^{\le n}(\mathfrak A)}\\
\Hom_{\mathsf{Ch}(\mathfrak A)}(X,Y)\simeq\Hom_{\mathsf{Ch}^{\ge n}(\mathfrak A)}(\tau^{\ge n}X,Y),\quad Y\in\operatorname{ob}{\mathsf{Ch}^{\ge n}(\mathfrak A)}
\end{aligned}$$
