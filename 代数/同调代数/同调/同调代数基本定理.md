#代数 

>[!thm] 同调代数基本定理 (Fundemental Theorem of Homological Algebra, FTHA)
>设 $0\to X^\bullet\xrightarrow fY^\bullet\xrightarrow gZ^\bullet\to0$ 是 $\mathsf{Ch}(\mathfrak A)$ 中的短正合列, 则由[[蛇形引理]]可以诱导 $\mathfrak A$ 中的典范[[正合序列]].
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd}
\begin{document}
\large
\begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large]
\cdots \arrow[r] & H^{n-1}(Y) \arrow[r, "{H^{n-1}(g)}" inner sep=0.7em] \arrow[phantom, d, ""{coordinate, name=A}] & H^{n-1}(Z) \arrow[dll, rounded corners, "{\delta^{n-1}}"', to path={
-- ([xshift=2ex]\tikztostart.east)
|- (A) [near end]\tikztonodes
-| ([xshift=-2ex]\tikztotarget.west)
-- (\tikztotarget)}] \\
H^n(X) \arrow[r, "{H^n(f)}"] & H^n(Y) \arrow[r, "{H^n(g)}"] \arrow[phantom, d, ""{coordinate, name=B}] & H^n(Z) \arrow[dll, rounded corners, "{\delta^n}"', to path={
-- ([xshift=2ex]\tikztostart.east)
|- (B) [near end]\tikztonodes
-| ([xshift=-2ex]\tikztotarget.west)
-- (\tikztotarget)}] \\
H^{n+1}(X) \arrow[r, "{H^{n+1}(f)}"' inner sep=0.7em] & H^{n+1}(Y) \arrow[r] & \cdots
\end{tikzcd}
\end{document}
>```
>并且若有 $\mathsf{Ch}(\mathfrak A)$ 中行正合的交换图表
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \large \begin{tikzcd} 0 & X & Y & Z & 0 \\ 0 & {\underline{X}} & {\underline{Y}} & {\underline{Z}} & 0 \arrow[from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["u", from=1-2, to=1-3] \arrow["f", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["v", from=1-3, to=1-4] \arrow["g", from=1-3, to=2-3] \arrow[from=1-4, to=1-5] \arrow["h", from=1-4, to=2-4] \arrow[from=2-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{\underline u}", from=2-2, to=2-3] \arrow["{\underline u}", from=2-3, to=2-4] \arrow[from=2-4, to=2-5] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
>```
>则它们给出交换图表
>```tikz
>\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \large \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small] \cdots & {H^{n-1}(Z)} & {H^n(X)} & {H^n(Y)} & {H^n(Z)} & {H^{n+1}(X)} & \cdots \\ \cdots & {H^{n-1}(\underline Z)} & {H^n(\underline X)} & {H^n(\underline Y)} & {H^n(\underline Z)} & {H^{n+1}(\underline X)} & \cdots \arrow[from=1-1, to=1-2] \arrow["{\delta^{n-1}}", from=1-2, to=1-3] \arrow["{H^{n-1}(h)}", from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow["{H^n(u)}", from=1-3, to=1-4] \arrow["{H^n(f)}", from=1-3, to=2-3] \arrow["{H^n(v)}", from=1-4, to=1-5] \arrow["{H^n(g)}", from=1-4, to=2-4] \arrow["{\delta^n}", from=1-5, to=1-6] \arrow["{H^n(h)}", from=1-5, to=2-5] \arrow[from=1-6, to=1-7] \arrow["{H^{n+1}(f)}", from=1-6, to=2-6] \arrow[from=2-1, to=2-2] \arrow["{\underline \delta^{n-1}}"', from=2-2, to=2-3] \arrow["{H^n(\underline u)}"', from=2-3, to=2-4] \arrow["{H^n(\underline v)}"', from=2-4, to=2-5] \arrow["{\underline\delta^n}"', from=2-5, to=2-6] \arrow[from=2-6, to=2-7] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
>```

>[!pf]
>对于每个 $n\in\Z$, 由 $\coker$ 作为函子的右正合性, 有正合列
>$$\coker d^n_X\to\coker d^n_Y\to\coker d^n_Z\to0$$
>类似地由 $\ker$ 的左正合性, 有正合列
>$$0\to\ker d^n_X\to\ker d^n_Y\to\ker d^n_Z$$
>我们可以将其置入交换图表
>```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd} \begin{document} \large \begin{tikzcd}[row sep=large] & {\mathrm{coker}\,d^{n-2}_X} & {\mathrm{coker}\,d^{n-2}_Y} & {\mathrm{coker}\,d^{n-2}_Z} & 0 \\ 0 & {\ker d^n_X} & {\ker d^n_Y} & {\ker d^n_Z} \arrow[from=1-2, to=1-3] \arrow[from=1-2, to=2-2] \arrow[from=1-3, to=1-4] \arrow[from=1-3, to=2-3] \arrow[from=1-4, to=1-5] \arrow[from=1-4, to=2-4] \arrow[from=2-1, to=2-2] \arrow[from=2-2, to=2-3] \arrow[from=2-3, to=2-4] \end{tikzcd} \end{document}
>```
>其中, 垂直箭头分别由 $d^{n-1}_X,d^{n-1}_Y,d^{n-1}_Z$ 诱导, 并且具有形如
>$$\coker d^{n-1}\xtwoheadrightarrow{d^{n-1}}\im d^{n-1}\hookrightarrow\ker d^n$$
>的满-单分解.
>
>其中我们可以从满射部分 $\coker d^{n-2}\twoheadrightarrow\im d^{n-1}$ 确定垂直箭头的 $\ker$, 由上同调的定义依次得到 $H^{n-1}(X),H^{n-1}(Y),H^{n-1}(Z)$. 同理从 $\im d^{n-1}\hookrightarrow\ker d^n$ 确定了余核, 这就确定了 $H^{n}(X),H^{n}(Y),H^{n}(Z)$. 由[[蛇形引理]], 这就得到了态射 $\delta^{n-1}:H^{n-1}(Z)\to H^n(X)$ 以及长正合列中涉及 $H^n$ 于 $H^{n-1}$ 的项. 函子性显然.
